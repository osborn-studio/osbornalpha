Things the spec gets right and must preserve:

- Explicit removal of mock financials, broker reports, and news from the company page; no proxy replacement for missing HK facts.
- Issuer-level tracking kept separate from ticker-based watchlists, with session-bound RLS, explicit CSRF checks, and `no-store` tracking responses.
- Public event/coverage tables intended to contain only public facts, with private judgments kept outside them.
- Available date distinguished from first observation time; disclosure histories and derived-input evidence treated as first-class UI.
- Table-first financial views, token-based styling, bilingual delivery, and explicit critical-suite registration.
- F3/F4 boundaries and honest empty states instead of inventing document or judgment capabilities.
