**What must not be regressed**

- Market-scoped fact streams, single-envelope reads, and fail-closed truncation.
- Atomic event/coverage replacement, semantic event identity, tombstones, and preserved first observation.
- Live disclosure-time replay, explicit replay exclusions, and separation of public facts from private user state.
- Removal of mock consensus/financial content; missing HK facts remaining missing.
- Correct DCF dimensional scaling, explicit economic basis, and factual seeds protected from LLM overwrite.
- Read-time freshness, RLS, private/no-store responses, table equivalents, bilingual registration, and critical database-suite registration.
