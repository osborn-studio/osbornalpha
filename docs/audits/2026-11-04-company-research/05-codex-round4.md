| 58 | P1 | §§4.4, 4.6, 6.3 | **An older success can clear a newer failure.** At revision R: begin attempt 1; begin attempt 2; attempt 2 fails and records backoff; attempt 1 succeeds. The replacement guard compares only against `last_success_attempt_id`, so attempt 1 passes and unconditionally resets failure state. | `backlogFailed` becomes zero even though no attempt newer than the failure recovered it. Retain suppression of failures superseded by a successful attempt. Add database tests for both completion orders: newer failure → older success, and newer success → older failure. |
