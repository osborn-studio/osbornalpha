The evidence-index adapter has several necessary failure rules:

- **Hash matching alone is insufficient.** Tushare hashes identify a source row; multiple concepts from that row share the hash (`providers/tushare-statements.ts:175–186`). Match concept, source field/expression, filing, period, unit and exact normalized value as well.
- EDGAR exposes the exact locator on parser candidates (`providers/edgar-companyfacts.ts:145–146`); it is not the JSON Pointer requested by the company DTO. Preserve both.
- Missing objects/sidecars, unsupported historical mappings, conflicting matches, parser drift or mismatched values must produce `evidence_incomplete`, never a guessed association.
- Sidecars are first-write metadata (`snapshot-store.ts:3–5,45–46`); repeated identical bytes do not prove the current run’s fetch time. Label snapshot capture time separately from run timing.
- Re-parsing Tushare snapshots cannot manufacture CN PDF page/table/cell verification. CN remains withheld until that independently verified association exists.
- The security-definer evidence reader must constrain eligible facts and whitelist output fields; elevated access must not expose staged/quarantined facts, tokens or internal batch details.
