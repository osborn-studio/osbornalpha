**Challenges to the second draft**

P1 = blocking correctness, recovery, or mandate issue. P2 = a contract that should be settled before implementation planning.

| # | Severity | Section | Issue | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | P1 | §5.2 | **SQL and prose select different current disclosures.** | Prose chooses the latest mapping **within each filing**, then orders disclosures. SQL sorts `mapping_version` ahead of disclosure date across the entire K. An older filing at mapping 2 beats a newer filing at mapping 1. | First select one mapping revision per filing/K; then select/order disclosures by disclosure date. Test mixed mapping versions across old and new filings. |
| N2 | P1 | §§4.2, 5.2–5.3 | **Mapping upgrades cannot reliably replace or remove previous interpretations.** | Text maximum is not version precedence: `facts-map/1.9` sorts above `facts-map/1.10`. A mapping that stops emitting a concept, changes its unit, or fixes its period leaves the old K visible forever. | Define ordered mapping generations and their activation scope, including explicit retirement/tombstones or complete replacement manifests. State whether quarantined new mappings permit fallback to older mappings. |
| N3 | P1 | §§4.1, 5.2–5.3, 6.3 | **Synthetic report types become fictional disclosure order.** | Conditional on type 1 and type 5 sharing an announcement date, lexical `source_ref` makes type 5 win over type 1. Equal-valued aliases also become two “disclosures” even if they refer to one actual document. Determinism alone does not establish chronology. | Separate adapter-row identity from evidenced disclosure identity. Collapse aliases only with evidence; mark unresolved same-date conflicts ambiguous rather than choosing by report-type string. Verify actual provider behavior first. |
| N4 | P2 | §§4.1–4.2, 4.7 | **The composite FK protects ownership, not all advertised invariants.** | It correctly prevents cross-issuer filing links, but permits `fact.available_date != filing.available_date`. Filings remain freely mutable by the service role. A status-only UPDATE trigger does not prevent INSERT directly as `ok`, bypassing staged publication. | Enforce date consistency and restrict filing identity/date mutation. Require staged inserts and route publication/status changes through narrowly defined operations, or explicitly document which invariants remain application-enforced. |
| N5 | P1 | §§4.4, 5.4, 6.1 | **The lease has no fencing after expiry.** | Worker A can exceed 600 seconds; B claims the issuer and deletes A’s staged rows; A resumes, inserts/publishes, and clears B’s lease. Publication checks neither ownership nor expiry, and release is unconditional. CLI execution explicitly has no deadline. | Use a lease token/generation, renewal, and ownership-checked release. Require a valid token for staging and publication; coordinate cleanup under the same protected ownership. |
| N6 | P1 | §§5.3–5.4, 6.1, 7.1 | **Crash recovery permanently orphans quarantine state.** | A crash after `staged→quarantined` but before `quarantine()` leaves facts that cleanup cannot delete and retries always skip. A crash after writing the queue item but before publishing can leave the rest of that filing absent while acceptance exposes only the quarantined subset. | Make rule disposition, queue associations, and publication recoverable as one issuer generation. Specify recovery for every interruption boundary, including non-staged rows from an unfinished generation. |
| N7 | P1 | §§5.2, 5.4, 9 | **Atomic UPDATE does not provide an atomic API read.** | Landed `selectAll` explicitly uses separate offset-paginated requests, not a snapshot. Publishing while reading can shift visible UUID-ordered pages, omitting or duplicating rows; separate concept reads can combine before/after states. This contradicts “reader sees none or the whole batch.” | Read through a single snapshot/RPC or pin all pages and concepts to a committed publication generation. Test publication during a multi-page read. |
| N8 | P1 | §§4.2, 4.6, 6.1–6.3, 8 | **Decimal fidelity is still only protected at the final projection.** | `value_text` preserves the numeric value PostgreSQL received, not digits already lost by JSON parsing or JS arithmetic. §6.1 explicitly reads `value`; landed DQ rules accept JS numbers. The 15-digit fixture does not test integers beyond JS’s exact range. | Specify lossless numeric parsing, decimal representation/arithmetic, rounding, and text reads throughout planning, rules, derivation, and persistence. Test unsafe integers and fractional arithmetic end to end. |
| N9 | P1 | §§3, 6.2, 8 | **Per-period fallback still changes accounting basis silently.** | `net_change_in_cash` switches between including and excluding restricted cash. CN `capex` includes intangible and other long-term assets, whereas the US mapping names PP&E acquisitions. Keeping `source_concept` does not stop these becoming apparently comparable facts or operands. [Tushare cash-flow fields](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=44). | Split materially different concepts or persist explicit basis qualifiers and enforce compatibility in formulas. Define fallback lists as equivalent measures, not merely acceptable substitutes. |
| N10 | P1 | §§1, 4.6, 6.1, 6.3, 9 | **CN publication still violates the primary-source/replay mandate.** | Publication precedes linking; linkage failure is informational; type 4/5 are excluded; PDFs are neither retained nor hashed, and no supporting page/row is established. `citation='linked'` merely means a URL exists. | Keep adapter-only/unverified rows outside public facts and derived metrics until required evidence exists. Retain verified document identity/hash and fact locator, or explicitly obtain a changed mandate before promising this delivery. |
| N11 | P1 | §6.3 | **The cninfo matching rule can cite the wrong document and cannot recover transient failures.** | It matches report words within ±3 days but not report year or exact correction identity. “First match” conflicts with “multiple indistinguishable means no link.” Corrected type-1 rows may need documents the title filter excludes. §6.1 retries only **new** filings. | Match issuer, reporting period, document kind, and correction/version evidence; inspect all candidate pages before declaring uniqueness. Persist retryable pending linkage for existing filings. Verify Beijing/category behavior separately—the landed collector only handles SH/SZ. |
| N12 | P1 | §§4.3, 6.1, 7.3, 8–9 | **Metric replacement can remain stale, partial, or empty—not merely briefly empty.** | Delete followed by chunked inserts exposes nonempty partial generations that §9 will cache. Failure after deletion persists until another rebuild. Two hooks/CLI/sync rebuilds can interleave, and an older computation can overwrite newer facts. Future-dated facts also become readable without a corresponding scheduled materialization. | Build a complete metric generation and atomically activate it with a source-generation check. Serialize or reject stale recomputations; define date-boundary refresh and recovery after failed rebuilds. |
| N13 | P1 | §§4.3, 8 | **Multi-currency ratios collide despite unit-aware facts.** | USD and EUR revenue/profit pairs can produce two margins for the same interval. Both outputs have `unit='ratio'`, so their primary keys collide. Consolidation and accounting/source basis are likewise absent. | Persist the input reporting currency and relevant basis dimensions in metric identity, or explicitly select one evidenced reporting basis before deriving. |
| N14 | P1 | §8 | **YTD differences can mix incompatible revisions.** | Independently selecting each K’s latest disclosure can combine a revised 9M total with an unrevised 6M total. Matching start date and currency cannot establish that their difference is a disclosed-compatible quarter. The upstream brief explicitly requires compatible financial-statement versions. | Define compatible revision/basis sets, with evidence for mixing filings. If compatibility cannot be established, omit the derived quarter rather than infer one. |
| N15 | P2 | §8 | **Period and lineage contracts remain ambiguous.** | Subtraction does not explicitly set quarter start to the subtrahend’s end plus one day. YoY matches duration and a 350–380-day end window, not a unique comparable interval. ROE’s opening-equity rule is a range rather than the exact window opening. TTM/ROE consume derived inputs, while output lineage only describes facts. A fixed two-year lookback is not proven sufficient for all allowed intervals. | Define exact derived intervals, unique dependency selection, ambiguity handling, opening-balance dates, dependency-driven lookback, and flattened fact lineage. |
| N16 | P1 | §§6.1, 7.1–7.3 | **Quarantine acceptance is not association-safe across mapping upgrades or retries.** | Quarantine natural keys omit mapping version. Landed `quarantine()` returns an existing open item without updating its payload; a new mapping generation can therefore quarantine new fact IDs that no queue item references. A failed hook retried after setting rows `ok` may also attempt a transition outside the trigger’s listed set. | Store/reconcile explicit quarantine-to-fact associations, scoped by generation. Update only still-quarantined eligible rows, respect all blocking decisions, and serialize hook retries with durable completion state. |
| N17 | P2 | §§7.1–7.3 | **Some “accepted” quarantines do nothing, and rule execution is underspecified.** | The first three reasons have no facts and acceptance is explicitly a no-op, providing no way to select an ambiguous candidate. §7.1’s literal `identityRule` calls omit the required `ruleId` argument. §7.2 aggregates warnings, but does not explicitly say aggregation spans the whole batch; CN warning denominators differ from §8. | Distinguish acknowledgement from fact promotion; provide a supported candidate-resolution path where promotion is intended. Specify the real helper signatures, batch-wide warning aggregation, and consistent applicable formulas. |
| N18 | P1 | §§4.4, 6.1, 6.4–6.5 | **`next_due_at` orders work but never makes work ineligible.** | Selection only requires `priority>0`. A small CN queue will be refreshed every four hours even when all rows are due next week, contradicting the stated weekly cadence. The update shorthand also fails to distinguish `last_success_at=now` from `next_due_at=now+7d`. | Select only `next_due_at <= now`, add an issuer tie-breaker, and specify explicit success/failure timestamps. Preserve schedule state during reconciliation. |
| N19 | P2 | §§4.4, 6.4, 8 | **One issuer still cannot have two independently scheduled feeds.** | The primary key is only `issuer_id`, with one market/fetch key. If an issuer qualifies for both US and CN ingestion, reconciliation must overwrite one. §8’s claim that US and CN revenue variants cannot coexist is not guaranteed by the issuer model. | Key jobs by issuer/source or explicitly exclude dual-feed ingestion with a documented coverage limitation. Define accounting-source selection for derivation. |
| N20 | P1 | §§6.1–6.3, 6.5, 12 | **The deadline does not bound an issuer’s work.** | Five possible 60-second SEC attempts plus backoff exceed 300 seconds even before storage and writes. An issuer started at second 199 can run far beyond the limit. `SyncDeps` has no propagated cancellation/deadline contract. | Propagate the remaining budget through fetch, retries, uploads, linking, and publication; stop retries when insufficient time remains. Bound/recover unfinished work and replace the 200-second completion acceptance claim with a demonstrable contract. |
| N21 | P1 | §6.3 | **Coverage checking reintroduces the first-observation/listing-date error.** | §2 acknowledges that `issuer_securities.valid_from` is first observation, but §6.3 exempts earlier years as pre-listing. Counting periods across all endpoints also cannot establish that each statement is complete. | Use evidenced listing/reporting eligibility dates, or report eligibility as unknown. Check coverage per endpoint/report type and distinguish missing periods from legitimately absent concepts. |
| N22 | P1 | §§4.8, 5.2, 6.1, 8 | **The proposed dependency boundary cannot execute the workflow.** | Landed `TableClient` has no `delete`, RPC, transaction, or projection method; `selectAll` accepts no ranges. Adding only `lte` and `insertMany` still cannot invoke either SQL function, clean staging, rebuild metrics, or perform the specified reads. `SyncDeps` supplies no alternative DB handle. | Specify narrow injected lease/publication/cleanup/materialization operations and a range-aware paged reader, with production, memory, and PostgreSQL implementations. | N24 | P2 | §§9, 4.6 | **API caching and citation fields do not fully describe what was answered.** | Historical responses resolve ticker ownership **today** but receive a 24-hour shared TTL, potentially spanning a reassignment boundary. | N25 | P1 | §§11.1, 11.3–11.5, 12, 14 | **The proposed acceptance gates cannot establish the promised two-market delivery.** | The critical offline-integration file contains a skippable CN block, but landed CI rejects **any** non-passed assertion in a critical file; there is no block-level exemption. The warning-summary change itself is sound: one batch-wide summary per rule avoids the original error-cap problem. The composite ownership FK is also a valid improvement. Neither fixes the adjacent recovery and consistency problems above.

External uncertainty remains material. I checked the official [Tushare balance-sheet](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=36), [cash-flow](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=44), and [SEC API](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) documentation. I **cannot substantiate** complete Tushare revision retention, announcement-date rewriting after corrections, or reliable `limit`/`offset`/`has_more` semantics for all three endpoints. Those need recorded responses and explicit failure behavior; §6.3 should not present them as established guarantees.

**Final verdict: not ready. Blocking items only:**

- Correct mapping/disclosure selection and same-date revision identity: **N1–N3**.
- Fence expired workers and make publication, quarantine recovery, and paginated reads consistent: **N5–N7, N16**.
- Define lossless numeric handling and compatible accounting/derivation bases: **N8–N9, N13–N14**.
- Enforce primary-document evidence and reliable, retryable cninfo linkage: **N10–N11**.
- Make metric generations recoverable and protected against stale concurrent writes: **N12**.
- Fix due eligibility, execution deadlines, and historical coverage checks: **N18, N20–N21**.
- Supply executable database-operation contracts: **N22**.
- Make the required two-market evidence and CI gates internally consistent: **N25**.
