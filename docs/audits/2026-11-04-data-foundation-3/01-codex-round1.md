Three suspected problems are **not inherently blockers**: PostgreSQL can update `valid_from` while rechecking exclusion constraints; Plan 2’s lifecycle trigger permits `ok→quarantined`; and a migration-owned `SECURITY DEFINER` function can update acceptance columns without granting direct updates to `service_role`. The surrounding contracts remain incomplete, as detailed below.
