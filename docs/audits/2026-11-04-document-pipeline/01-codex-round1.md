The design is not implementable as written. Its re-extraction model cannot preserve citations, private responses inherit public cache headers, upload identity is not tenant-scoped, and HK promotion can publish facts before their evidence exists. The HK parser also lacks essential period, unit, and sign semantics. These are contract and data-model defects, not implementation details. The dependency baseline is stale: the repository’s Plan 2 spec is now **draft 7**, and F3’s proposed independent landing order conflicts with the migration-history gate. This review was read-only; no files were changed or tests run. Verdict: not ready.
