The fourth draft fixes per-association finalization, approval-version lookup, historical-read expectations, and same-generation staging transitions. It still has blocking contract gaps: recovery attempts abandonment without a lease, publication validates mutable evidence bindings without protecting them through commit, and inherited confirmations cannot receive their required checks. The new selectors also leave retry paths inconsistent. This review was read-only and excludes previously resolved findings and external OPEN-verify items. Verdict: not ready.
