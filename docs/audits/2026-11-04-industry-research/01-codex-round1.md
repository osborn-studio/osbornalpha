**Verdict:** **Not ready for implementation planning.** The architecture has useful boundaries, but its central promises—replayable numbers, point-in-time membership, controlled publication, and reconciled valuations—are not supported by the proposed contracts. The biggest blockers are digest-only lineage, historical membership resolved through today’s issuers, an unusable historical SIC fallback, licensing gates bypassed by database grants, and valuation keys that cannot support the specified publication lifecycle. The checkout’s plan-two spec is **draft 7**, so this spec’s draft-three dependency assumptions also need reconciliation. No files were modified.

The SQL review distinguishes actual defects from shorthand: PostgreSQL supports [partial unique indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/indexes-partial.html) and [stored generated columns](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-generated-columns.html). The blockers are lifecycle, constraints and permissions, not the mere use of those features.

**Keep these decisions in revision**

- Overall-method aggregation, matched-set YoY, explicit units, and financial-sector exceptions.
- Missing values and coverage failures remain visible; no neutral placeholders or proxy cash-flow metrics.
- Financial ratios and empirical percentiles remain distinct from lifecycle/valuation judgments.
- Source drawers, bilingual messages, table-equivalent charts, and explicit extension of the hard-coded i18n parity tests.
- Raw-response preservation, real-role database tests, and an explicit licensing decision before release.

For scope control, defer F4 consumer wiring, judgment placeholders and the 31×10 competitor regression exercise until the evidence/publication contracts work. Historical identity, public ingestion scope and immutable lineage are day-one dependencies, not optional extensions.

**Explicit “unsure” list**

- **`index_member_all`:** I could not verify that `is_new=N` plus `Y` covers all historical transitions, that pagination uses reliable `limit/offset/has_more`, whether `out_date` is inclusive, or what pre-2021 membership dates mean. The [official documentation](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=335) establishes fields and the 2,000-row cap, not those guarantees.
- **`index_classify`:** The [official documentation](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=181) distinguishes `industry_code` and `index_code`, but I could not verify the returned `parent_code` namespace against a live payload. Directly copying it into an FK keyed by `index_code` needs a fixture check.
- **`sw_daily`:** Unsure about TTM/static PE, loss-company treatment, equity adjustments, share-class treatment, historical revisions and methodology continuity across classification versions. These determine whether 1% reconciliation is meaningful.
- **`daily_basic`:** Unsure about historical revision/availability guarantees, multi-class `total_share` semantics, suspension coverage, and pagination behavior. Empty results must not automatically establish a non-trading day.
- **SEC submissions/SIC:** The live AAPL JSON was inaccessible through the browsing tool, so I did not independently verify the spec’s sample field values. The [SEC SIC list](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/standard-industrial-classification-sic-code-list) is available and includes three-digit displayed codes, requiring explicit zero-padding. Neither that list nor the [API overview](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) establishes a historical SIC series or the proposed `1987` dataset-version contract.
- **CSRC:** The [listed archive](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100103/common_list.shtml) ends at 2021Q3, and its [release page](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100103/c1558619/content.shtml) is dated 2021-11-10. I did not verify every PDF or establish that no newer classification results exist elsewhere. CSRC-to-SW conceptual comparison cannot validate SW historical membership.
- **Licensing:** Commercial display, redistribution, raw retention and admin-only use rights for the Tushare/SW datasets remain unverified. API access entitlement alone does not settle those rights.
