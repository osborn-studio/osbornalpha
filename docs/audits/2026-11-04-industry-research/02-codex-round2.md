**Verdict:** **Not ready for implementation planning.** Round 2 materially improves the design, especially SIC scope, plan-2 draft-8 alignment, transactional membership writes, and per-industry reconciliation. However, historical membership still relies on unverified knowledge dates and today’s identity resolution; the new issuer loader cannot use the landed identifier API as specified; incremental reuse and manifest retention can produce stale or unreplayable results; and replay can bypass valuation publication gates. The revision also omits essential aggregate-table definitions and introduces a freshness value rejected by the landed database. No files were modified.

The revision should preserve these decisions:

- SIC is current classification/navigation only.
- Overall-method aggregation, matched-set YoY, explicit units and financial-sector exceptions.
- Annual-only CN gross margin until supported quarterly derivations exist.
- Immutable evidence, separate original replay/current recomputation, complete publication snapshots, and real-role database tests.
- Per-industry/per-metric reconciliation, no quota-driven relaxation, and no public PS without verification.
- Missing-data states, bilingual UI, table-equivalent charts, and separation of statistics from judgments.

The [official documentation](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=335) establishes fields and a 2,000-row cap, not those guarantees.
- **`index_classify`:** Unsure about `parent_code` namespace in live responses. The [official page](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=181) distinguishes industry and index codes; fixture verification remains necessary.
- **`sw_daily`:** Unsure about static versus TTM PE, loss-company treatment, equity adjustments, share-class treatment and historical methodology continuity. The [official documentation](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=327) labels PE/PB but does not establish comparator equivalence.
- **`daily_basic`:** Unsure about H/B inclusion in total shares, historical correction availability, suspension coverage and pagination guarantees. Its [documented units](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=32) support the ×10,000 conversion, not the full valuation interpretation.
- **SEC submissions/SIC:** The live AAPL submissions URL was inaccessible through the browsing tool; I could not independently confirm the sample field values. The [SEC SIC list](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/standard-industrial-classification-sic-code-list) supports code normalization, not a historical membership series.
- **CSRC:** The [referenced archive](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100103/common_list.shtml) lists 2021Q3 as its newest entry, dated November 10, 2021. I did not establish that no newer publications exist elsewhere or verify every PDF. It cannot establish SW knowledge as of September 30.
- **cninfo/licensing:** Unsure whether `topSearch` supplies stable, exhaustive historical issuer identity across exchanges and ticker changes. Commercial display, redistribution, raw retention and internal-use rights for the adapter datasets also remain unverified.
