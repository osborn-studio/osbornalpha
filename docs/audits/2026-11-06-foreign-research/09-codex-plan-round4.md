| **P-R4-01** | **P2** | **A2, L2089, L2097–2100** | **The replacement privilege inventory still fails at runtime.** `pg_get_function_identity_arguments` includes parameter names, producing signatures such as `public.broker_document_claim_lease(p_document uuid, p_token uuid, p_ttl_seconds integer)`.
