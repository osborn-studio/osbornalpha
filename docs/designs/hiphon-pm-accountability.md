## Vision

### 10x Check
v1 is "an AI that gives investment advice when asked." The 10x version is
"an AI PM with a track record you can trust because it's on the record."
Every call John Cameron makes gets logged and scored against what the market
actually did. After 3 months you don't have opinions, you have a hit rate.
That is the cure for the two industry diseases (overfit backtests, overconfident
advice) and the thing no benchmark-chasing competitor has.

### Platonic Ideal
8am: John Cameron has written today's call sheet. Overnight macro change, actions
on YOUR actual holdings, 1-2 fresh buys with size and stop, and "my last 30 days:
18 calls, 61% hit, +4.2% avg on buys." Every number clickable to source. He
remembers last week's calls and says "I was wrong on X, here's why, I'm cutting."
A partner who is accountable, not a chatbot who forgets.

### Eureka (logged)
The edge is not a smarter picking LLM. It is the model-as-analyst reasoning over a
proprietary, real-time, China-deep data layer (北向 / tushare / 东方财富) with a
disciplined process and a self-scoring track record. The moat is pipeline + process
+ accountability. The pipeline already exists.

## Landscape (why this premise is right)
- LLMs are systematically wrong outside their home info environment; generic LLMs are
  more bullish + higher error on Chinese stocks (HBR 2026, A-share study).
- AI pickers overfit: 20-50% live decay from backtest. SEC/FINRA/NASAA warn AI advice
  sounds overconfident vs evidence.
- Therefore: accountability + calibration + China-deep data provenance is the wedge.
