## Parser contract

`parsePbcAfreStockArticle` is a standalone offline function, intentionally absent from the active registry and all collectors. It accepts fatal UTF-8 bytes (1–262144 bytes) and a caller-supplied receipt. Three exact verified HTTPS article URLs are allowlisted with their own year/month and publication date; no redirects, network fetch or arbitrary source IDs are accepted. The caller must establish that bytes came from that URL; receipt validation is not network authenticity verification.

The function requires unique ArticleTitle/PubDate metadata, the exact title bound to that URL’s verified observation month, its exact verified publication date after period end and no later than retrieval's UTC date, and exactly one headline paragraph. A strict headline grammar binds period, total stock in 万亿元 and an explicit published percent growth/decline. Stock-report paragraph bodies must be plain text. Version 4 additionally permits plain, numbered `<strong>` section-heading paragraphs only at the newly verified combined-report URL; a section heading containing `社会融资规模存量为`, entities, nested tags, or inline headline markup is rejected to avoid concealed duplicate headlines. Unsupported layout, encoded/inline headline markup, missing values, wrong units, flow titles, mismatched months or duplicate candidates throw. Comments/scripts/styles are masked while preserving character offsets. This deliberately narrow grammar is not a general HTML parser or an unrestricted historical archive contract. Version `pbc-afre-stock-article/2` adds July and binds both publications to their verified dates; corrected values at the same publication date remain separate byte evidence, while a changed publication date requires source re-verification.

Output is a research candidate, **not** `ParsedPoint`/`ObservationWrite`: fixed series ID, month end, canonical decimal percent text (8.8, not 0.088), unit, `revisable: true`, publication date, retrieval timestamp, source URL, parser version, full-byte SHA-256 and UTF-16 value range into the decoded evidence. The range is not an ingest Locator. Never recompute growth from rounded stock balances. A changed value yields changed evidence; no observation version or automatic supersession is assigned. Published date never substitutes for first-seen availability.

Version `pbc-afre-stock-article/3` copies only `sourceUrl` and `retrievedAt` from the receipt. Structurally compatible callers may carry additional admission metadata; those extra properties are ignored, so they cannot overwrite validated article fields or add availability/ingest fields to the candidate. July and August regression cases demonstrate this boundary with conflicting publication, period, value, series and unit metadata. The verified sources and publication checks are unchanged.
