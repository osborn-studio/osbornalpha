# Report Contract

Every research book is a directory, not a disposable answer.

```text
research-books/<company>/<period>/
  sources/
  references/
    source-inventory.md
    quote-ledger.md
    missing-sources.md
  chapters/
    00_executive_summary.md
    01_business_and_segments.md
    02_financial_quality.md
    03_management_claims.md
    04_catalysts_and_risks.md
    05_valuation_inputs.md
    06_open_questions.md
  exports/
```

Minimum book sections:

1. entity resolution
2. source inventory
3. business and segment map
4. financial quality
5. management commentary and quote ledger
6. moat and competition
7. catalysts
8. risks and falsification triggers
9. valuation inputs
10. open questions
