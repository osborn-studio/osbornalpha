# Source Policy

Use primary sources first:

- earnings release
- 10-Q, 10-K, annual report, quarterly report, or exchange filing
- earnings-call transcript
- investor presentation
- official press release

Medium sources such as reputable financial media and broker summaries can frame questions, but they do not replace primary evidence.

For paid or user-provided private research, summarize only for the user's private workflow and preserve metadata. Do not reproduce long paid passages.

Every material claim needs:

- source title
- source class
- date
- retrieval note or file path
- claim label: confirmed evidence | management claim | inference | speculation
