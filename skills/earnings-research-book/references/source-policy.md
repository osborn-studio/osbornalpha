# Source Policy

## Priority Sources

1. Company investor relations pages.
2. SEC filings and exhibits.
3. Official earnings release, prepared remarks, transcript, and investor deck.
4. CNBC, Bloomberg, Reuters, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, and official webcast interviews.
5. Sell-side research from Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan, Bank of America, UBS, Evercore, Bernstein, Mizuho, Needham, KeyBanc, TD Cowen, Citi, and Jefferies when lawfully available.

## Required Source Handling

- Download source PDFs or source files into `sources/`.
- Keep English quotations in the report and references database.
- Record every source in `references/references.json`.
- Generate `references/references.bib` for downstream citation workflows.
- If a source cannot be downloaded, record a TODO reference with reason and retrieval owner.

## Copyright And Redistribution

- Use short quotations only where necessary for analysis.
- Do not paste full paid analyst reports into customer-visible output unless rights are confirmed.
- Public-company filings, press releases, and official IR decks may be cited, but attribution is still required.
- Separate facts, management claims, analyst interpretation, and the author's investment analysis.
