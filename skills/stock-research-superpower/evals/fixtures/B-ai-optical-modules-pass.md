# Workflow B: AI Optical Modules

## Scope

Workflow B, industry/theme research. Theme: AI optical modules. Geography: global with A/HK/US ticker resolution. Horizon: 6-18 months.

## Chain map

End demand: AI datacenter scale-out and end demand for 800G to 1.6T links.

Layers:

1. End demand / deployment: cloud AI clusters.
2. System integrator / OEM: network equipment and AI server platforms.
3. Subsystems: optical modules and switching systems.
4. Core components: DSP, EML, silicon photonics, lasers, TIA, drivers.
5. Manufacturing / assembly / test: yield, burn-in, qualification, reliability test.
6. Equipment / tooling: optical test and packaging tools.
7. Specialty materials: substrates, lenses, fiber components.
8. Raw inputs: wafers and specialty chemicals.

## Bottleneck

Bottleneck: high-speed optical engine qualification and capacity.

Constraint type: capacity, qualification, yield, and ramp difficulty.

Evidence status: inference until confirmed by primary filings or official transcripts. Primary, medium, and weak sources should be separated. missing-source: latest official capacity data.

Consensus awareness: medium. Confirm with customer qualification language, substitutability checks, and management claim review.

## Evidence

Evidence ledger:

- Claim: demand for optical links rises with AI cluster scale. Source class: medium until official hyperscaler capex source is attached. Interpretation: inference.
- Claim: qualification limits near-term substitution. Source class: unknown. missing-source: customer qualification disclosure.
- Claim: capacity can become tight during 1.6T ramp. Source class: unknown. missing-source: latest earnings transcript.

## What would break the thesis

The thesis weakens if demand shifts away from pluggable optics faster than expected, if new capacity ramps smoothly, if qualification cycles shorten, or if consensus already fully prices the bottleneck.
